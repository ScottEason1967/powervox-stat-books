Personal data we collect about you

We collect and use the following personal data about you:

  • your name and contact information, including your email address
  • your account details, being your email address used as your sign-in and a securely hashed password (we cannot see your password)
  • your billing information, being a reference to your customer record and subscription status with our payment provider, Stripe — your card details are collected and held by Stripe and never reach our systems
  • the contents of the statutory registers you build, which include information about your company's officers, members and persons with significant control: names, service addresses, shareholdings, dates of birth (month and year, as published by Companies House) and appointment dates — most of which originates from the public record at Companies House, with some added by you
  • details of any information, feedback or other matters you send us by email
  • basic technical information about your use of the service, such as access times, errors and IP addresses, held briefly in service logs

You must provide your email address and a password to use the service, and payment details (to Stripe) to subscribe. The contents of your registers are under your control: the service builds them from the public record and you choose what to add, correct or remove.

A point specific to this service: the registers you keep will usually contain personal data about other people, such as your company's directors and shareholders. Those registers are your company's own statutory records, which the law requires the company to hold, and your company is the controller of them in its own right. We process them on your instructions in order to provide the service.

A legitimate interest is when we have a business or commercial reason to use your personal data, so long as this is not overridden by your own rights and interests.

How long your personal data will be kept

We will not keep your personal data for longer than we need it for the purpose for which it is used. In particular: your account details and registers are kept while your account exists; if your subscription ends, they are retained so you can return, and deleted or anonymised twelve months after your subscription ends, or sooner if you ask; billing records are kept for six years as tax law requires; and service logs are kept for no more than thirty days. Following the end of the relevant retention period, we will delete or anonymise your personal data.

Transferring your personal data out of the UK

Countries outside the UK have differing data protection laws, some of which may provide lower levels of protection of privacy. Your registers and account data are stored at rest in London. However, some of our providers are US-headquartered, and it is sometimes necessary for personal data to be processed in, or accessible from, countries outside the UK, including the United States.

Where that happens, we do so only as UK data protection law permits: on the basis of a UK adequacy regulation (including the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for US providers certified under it), or where appropriate safeguards are in place such as legally-approved standard data protection clauses under Article 46(2) of UK GDPR, together with enforceable rights and effective legal remedies for you. If we cannot rely on one of those mechanisms, we will not transfer your personal data outside the UK unless an alternative lawful mechanism or exception applies and this policy has been updated to say so. For more information about our transfer safeguards, contact us using the details below.

Cookies and similar storage

Our service does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and does not track you. The only things stored in your browser are strictly necessary for the service to function: your sign-in session, and a local working copy of your registers so your book loads quickly and survives connection problems. Because we use only strictly necessary storage, no cookie consent banner is required. Stripe's payment pages, which are hosted by Stripe, use cookies of their own as described in Stripe's privacy policy.
