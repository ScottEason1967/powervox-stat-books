Privacy Notice

1. Who is responsible for your data

Eason Law Limited (company number 12308593, registered office 128 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX) is the data controller for the personal data processed by the Statutory Registers service at registers.easonlaw.co.uk. Contact: scott.eason@easonlaw.co.uk.

2. What we hold and why

Your account. Your email address and a securely hashed password, held so you can sign in and so we can contact you about your subscription. Legal basis: performing our contract with you.

Your billing. Payments are handled by Stripe. We hold a reference to your Stripe customer record and your subscription status; your card details are held by Stripe and never reach our systems. Legal basis: performing our contract, and our legal obligations regarding accounts and tax.

Your registers. The statutory registers you build contain information about your company's officers, members and persons with significant control — names, service addresses, shareholdings and appointment dates. Most of this originates from the public record at Companies House; some you may add yourself. We store it so the Service can maintain your books. Legal basis: performing our contract. For the people named in your registers, you should be aware that the registers you keep are your company's own statutory records, which the law requires the company to hold.

Service logs. Basic technical logs (times, errors, IP addresses) kept briefly for security and to fix faults. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure service.

3. Who processes it for us

We use a small number of carefully chosen providers, each acting on our instructions: Supabase (account sign-in and database hosting), Stripe (payments), and Vercel (website and application hosting). Company information is retrieved from the Companies House public data service. Some of these providers are US-headquartered; where personal data leaves the UK, it does so under recognised safeguards, including the UK's data bridge and international data transfer agreements.

4. What we don't do

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for marketing without your consent. The Service uses no advertising or analytics cookies: the only things stored in your browser are your sign-in session and a local working copy of your registers, both essential to the Service, which is why no cookie banner pesters you.

5. How long we keep it

Your account and registers are kept while your account exists. If you cancel, they are retained so you can return, and deleted twelve months after your subscription ends, or sooner if you ask. Billing records are kept for six years, as tax law requires. You can ask us to delete your account and registers at any time.

6. Your rights

You have the rights UK data protection law gives you: to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, to object to or restrict processing, and to receive a copy in a portable form. Write to scott.eason@easonlaw.co.uk and we will respond within a month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

7. Changes to this notice

If we change this notice in any meaningful way, we will tell you by email before the change takes effect.
